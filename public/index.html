How it works

Decisions stay with the coach. The feeling of being part of them goes to the fans.

1

A decision moment opens

Gemini follows the match events and opens a short, forward-looking question at the right minute.

2

Fans answer in seconds

One tap and, if they like, one line of reasoning in any language. In the stadium or on a second screen at home.

3

Gemini writes the Fan Pulse

All answers and reasons, in English, German, Turkish and more, become one live summary and three reason clusters.

4

The coach decides, Fan IQ lands

Fans whose call matches the coach earn Fan IQ points. Points unlock scarves, jerseys and experiences.

What makes it special

Rewards for loyalty and football knowledge, not for spending. And a data source the club owns.

Fan IQ

+10 when your call matches the coach's decision, +5 bonus when you were in the minority and still right. Never negative.

Loyalty weight

Every home match attended this season adds 0.05 to your vote weight, up to 2.0×. A reason to come to the stadium and to open the app.

Fan Insight Pool

Every resolved moment is stored anonymised and aggregated. Any department can ask the pool a question in plain language through Gemini.

The two-minute demo

  1. Open the operator panel and press “Start match”.
  2. Open the fan view on a phone (scan the code in the operator panel) or in a second tab.
  3. Press “Fire next decision moment”, vote on the phone, and watch the bars and the Fan Pulse update.
  4. Enter what the coach actually did and press “Resolve”. Fan IQ points land on the profile.
  5. Open the club dashboard: the same moment with breakdowns, now stored in the Fan Insight Pool. Ask the pool a question.

Three deliberate choices

The idea is built to fit the club's values and to be realistic to run.

Positive questions only

No fan is ever asked to rate a player negatively or to vote someone off. Questions look forward: who comes on, what changes, who impressed.

The coach always decides

Fans predict and voice their opinion. Nothing a fan taps changes what happens on the pitch.

Anonymised and consent-based

The club sees totals and summaries per decision moment, never an individual fan's answers. Fans can see what their answers are used for.

First test

The first step of our test plan is a one-match pilot with a closed group. We have started on campus.

First run: on campus, with a student club

We built this lightweight web prototype (Gemini API plus a simple live-vote backend) and ran it at our university together with a student club, with decision moments triggered manually by an operator.

What we measure

  1. Participation per decision moment and response time.
  2. Repeat participation across the 90 minutes.
  3. Whether fans rate the Gemini summary as a fair reflection of their opinion.
  4. Whether club teams find insights in the pool they would act on.

Next step

The same setup with 200–500 volunteers from official fan clubs during one home match, with five to six decision moments. If more than half of the participants answer at least three moments and the club's content and fan-relations teams find usable insights, the next step is a full stadium block.
