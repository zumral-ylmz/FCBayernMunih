What we took out

Some things don't belong in a stadium.

Votes against players.

No fan is ever asked to rate a player down or vote someone off. Every question looks forward: who comes on, what changes, who impressed.

Fans overruling the coach.

The coach always decides. Fans predict and say what they think. Being right is the reward.

Anyone's individual answers.

The club sees totals and summaries. Never what one fan said. Anonymised, aggregated, with consent.

First test

First run: on campus, with a student club

We built this lightweight web prototype (Gemini API plus a simple live-vote backend) and ran it at our university together with a student club, with Decision Moments triggered manually by an operator.

What we measure

  1. Participation per Decision Moment and response time.
  2. Repeat participation across the 90 minutes.
  3. Whether fans rate the Fan Pulse as fair.
  4. Whether club teams find insights they would act on.

Next step

The same setup with 200–500 volunteers from official fan clubs during one home match, with five to six Decision Moments. If more than half answer at least three moments and the club's teams find usable insights, the next step is a full stadium block.
